Reply Drafter

Post text (used as context)

Open a Facebook post, then press Scan comments. Tip: you don't need this panel for single replies. Click into any reply box on Facebook and use the ✨ AI reply button or a snippet right there.

Saved replies. Fill types a snippet into the reply box open on Facebook (if none is open, it copies instead). Picking a snippet on a comment card opens that comment's reply box and fills it. {name} becomes the commenter's first name.
